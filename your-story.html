Your Story

This whole site is built on one idea, which is that getting cut off at some level is what happens to almost everybody who plays. I can say that all day, but it hits way harder coming from a bunch of different people than it does coming from just me. So if you stopped competing at some point, I want to hear how it went.

Tell me what level you made it to, when you stopped, and what you ended up doing after. It does not have to be a sad story or a happy one, just a real one. If you check the box at the bottom, I might feature yours on the site down the road. I will never post anything without that permission.

Send In Your Story

This is a class project, so the form does not actually send anywhere yet. If you really want to share something, you can email me instead.
