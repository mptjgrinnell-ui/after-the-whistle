Keep Playing

Once you stop competing nobody hands you a schedule anymore, so the first thing that goes is just playing at all. You had practice set for you since you were like eight and then one day you have to go find a game yourself. The good news is there is way more out there than people think, you just have to pick the one that fits your life right now and not the one that fits the old you.

What actually decides it for most people who work a real job is money and time. So instead of writing a paragraph about every option I put them side by side. Find the row that fits your week and start there.

Ways to keep playing, compared
Option Roughly what it costs How much time it takes What you need to start
Organized adult rec league Around $80 to $150 a season per player One night a week for about eight weeks A team, or sign up solo and get placed on one
City parks and rec league Usually less than a private league, depends on your city One or two nights a week during the season Register on your city's parks and rec site before the deadline
Pickup or open gym Free most of the time, sometimes a small gym fee Whenever you can show up Shoes, a ball, and knowing when people are actually there
Adult tennis ladder Free or a small entry fee, plus balls About one match a week that you schedule yourself A racket and being willing to lose to strangers at first

Prices are ballpark numbers and they change a lot by city, so check the actual league before you count on them.

What It Looks Like Now

This is a pickup game filmed from way above. No refs, portable goals, a field that is half dirt, and it still looks like everybody is having a good time. That is kind of the whole point of this page. The level dropped but the game is still the game.

How To Actually Pick One

  • If you miss having a team and a schedule, go rec league. It is the closest thing to what you had.
  • If your work week is all over the place, pickup is better since nobody is counting on you to show.
  • If you played a one on one sport like tennis, a ladder gives you real matches without needing a whole team.
  • If you are coming off an injury, start with pickup so you can sub out whenever you want. The Injuries page gets into that more.

If you want to just look at what is out there, Volo Sports runs adult leagues in a lot of cities and you can sign up solo.
