The Last Game Nobody Warns You About

Almost nobody gets a goodbye game. For me it was never one big injury that ended it, it was a bunch of small ones stacking up over the years until my body just stopped letting me do the thing I had been doing since I was a kid. You rehab, you come back, you tweak something else, you rehab that. At some point you realize the math is not working anymore and you are done competing, except no one ever actually says it out loud to you.

The part that got me was how fast everything else disappears with it. Your schedule, your team, the reason you were in the weight room at six in the morning, all of that goes at the same time. I did the rehab and I play at the rec level now, which honestly is still really good, but it took me way too long to figure out that was even an option. That is the whole reason this site exists. Getting cut off somewhere is the normal outcome and not some personal failure, and there is a lot left after it.
